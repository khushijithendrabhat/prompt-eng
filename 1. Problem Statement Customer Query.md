1. Problem Statement: Customer Query Classification Using Prompt Engineering
An e-commerce company receives numerous customer queries related to orders,
deliveries, payments, refunds, cancellations, and product defects. Your task is to design
an effective prompt that classifies each customer query into the appropriate predefined
category.
Input: A customer query in natural language.
Output: A single word representing the category of the query.
Categories:
• Delivery
• Shipping
• Refund
• Payment
• Cancellation
• Defect
• Return
• Account
• Unknown
Examples:

Input Query Expected Output

My order has not arrived yet. Delivery
My package is stuck in transit. Shipping
I haven't received my money back. Refund
Money was deducted twice from my account. Payment
I want to cancel my order. Cancellation
The product I received is broken. Defect
I want to send this product back. Return
I cannot log in to my account. Account
What is the capital of India? Unknown

Constraint: The output must contain exactly one category word, without explanations,
punctuation, or additional text.



PROMPT:
You are a customer support assistant for an e-commerce company.

Your task is to read the customer's query and identify what it is about.

Classify the query into one of these categories:

Delivery
Shipping
Refund
Payment
Cancellation
Defect
Return
Account
Unknown

Choose the category that best matches the customer's issue.

If the query is not related to any of these categories, choose Unknown.

Important: Return only the category name. Do not give any explanation or extra text.

Examples:

Customer Query: My order has not arrived yet. Output: Delivery

Customer Query: My package is stuck in transit. Output: Shipping

Customer Query: I haven't received my money back. Output: Refund

Customer Query: Money was deducted twice from my account. Output: Payment

Customer Query: I want to cancel my order. Output: Cancellation

Customer Query: The product I received is broken. Output: Defect

Customer Query: I want to send this product back. Output: Return

Customer Query: I cannot log in to my account. Output: Account

Customer Query: What is the capital of India? Output: Unknown

Now classify this customer query:

Customer Query: [Enter customer query here]

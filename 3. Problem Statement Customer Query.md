customer requests, the company wants an AI system that identifies the urgency level of
each query.
Your task is to design an effective prompt that analyzes a customer query and classifies
its urgency.
Input: A customer query in natural language.
Output: A single word representing the urgency level.
Allowed Outputs:
• High
• Medium
• Low

Classify every query into one of these three levels:

High
Medium
Low

High: Choose this when the customer has a serious problem that needs immediate attention.
Medium: Choose this when the issue needs attention but is not an emergency.
Low: Choose this when the customer is asking a general question or making a simple request.

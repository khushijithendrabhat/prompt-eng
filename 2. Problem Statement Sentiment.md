2. Problem Statement: Sentiment Analysis
An online shopping company wants to analyze customer reviews automatically to
understand customer satisfaction. Your task is to design a prompt that identifies the
sentiment expressed in a customer review.
Classify each review into one of three categories based on the customer's overall
sentiment.
Input: A customer review in natural language.
Output: A single word representing the sentiment.
Allowed Outputs:
• Positive
• Negative
• Neutral

Examples:
Input Review Expected Output
The product is excellent, and I love its quality. Positive
The delivery was late, and the product was damaged. Negative
The product arrived yesterday. Neutral
The customer support team was extremely helpful. Positive
The product is not worth the money. Negative
The package contained two items. Neutral
The phone is good, but the battery life is terrible. Negative

Constraint: The output must contain exactly one word: Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
Do not provide explanations or additional text.



PROMPT:
You are a sentiment analysis assistant for an online shopping company.

Your task is to read customer reviews and identify the sentiment behind them.

Classify each review into one of these three categories:

Positive
Negative
Neutral

Choose Positive if the customer is happy or satisfied with the product or service.

Choose Negative if the customer is unhappy or mentions a problem.

Choose Neutral if the review only shares information without expressing any positive or negative opinion.

Important: Return only one word: Positive, Negative, or Neutral. Do not provide any explanation or extra text.

Examples:

Review: The product is excellent, and I love its quality. Output: Positive

Review: The delivery was late, and the product was damaged. Output: Negative

Review: The product arrived yesterday. Output: Neutral

Review: The customer support team was extremely helpful. Output: Positive

Review: The product is not worth the money. Output: Negative

Review: The package contained two items. Output: Neutral

Review: The phone is good, but the battery life is terrible. Output: Negative
